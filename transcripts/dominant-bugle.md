Others, though, just love getting a bull all fired up.

And believe me, I get it.

It's a rush screaming at a bull and having him screaming back at you as he comes charging in.

That's the sexy side of elk hunting.

But while there are some bulls out there that will vigorously and aggressively defend their harems, or that are attracted to the vocalizations of another dominant-sounding bull, like we discussed in the beginning, there are many that are not.

Not all bulls are the right bulls to use dominant bugles with.

Like the Maslow quote, "If all you have is a hammer, all you start seeing are nails."

If you approach every bull, or even just most bulls, with the intent on using dominant bugles, what you're effectively doing is looking for a bull that wants to play your game, rather than finding a bull and figuring out how to play his game.

In areas where you have good numbers of bulls to work with, and where those bulls are scattered out across the landscape, choosing a tactic like challenging a bull using dominant bugles and looking for a bull that wants to play your game can absolutely yield success, especially if you're out there hunting over multiple days or weeks.

The more bulls that you encounter over a longer period of time, the greater the likelihood of you running into the right bull that wants to play your game.

However, if you're hunting in an area with relatively few bulls, or where bulls are concentrated and pressured both by other bulls and other hunters, again, just be careful of jumping right into using dominant bugles and trying to challenge every bull that you hear.

For me, when I'm hunting areas like these, I typically start off with more passive or targeted strategies and tactics first, and then bump up to more aggressive tactics when all else fails, or if I happen to find myself in the specific situation that calls for a more aggressive approach.

But if you use them in the right situations, at the right time, with a strategic delivery, dominant bugles can sometimes be exactly what you need to pull that her bull right into your lap.
